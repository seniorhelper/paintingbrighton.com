Direct answerLochbuie, CO

Painter Hotline paints homes in Lochbuie, Colorado (ZIP 80603), the town just northeast of Brighton that straddles the Adams and Weld county line. Most Lochbuie houses date from the 2000s and 2010s and still wear their original builder paint, so the usual job is a first full exterior repaint, interior repaints, fence staining and HOA color requests. We charge no travel fee for Lochbuie, estimates are free, and the work carries a written 5-year workmanship warranty. Call 720-208-5645.

Lochbuie's homes, by the numbers

Census estimates count about 3,060 homes in Lochbuie. Roughly 1,280 were built in 2000–2009 and another 875 in 2010–2019, together about 71% of the town. Only about 17% predate 1980, far below Brighton's 32.5%. The town has also kept growing, from 8,104 residents at the 2020 Census to an estimated 8,599 in mid-2024.

Sources: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2019–2023 5-year estimates (tables B25034, B25003); Vintage 2024 population estimates. Compare with Brighton.

What that means for your paint

A whole street on the same clock

When hundreds of houses are built in the same few years with the same builder paint, they fade together. If the neighbors are getting their houses painted, yours is probably due too. Rub the south or west wall: a chalky palm means the binder has broken down and the house needs a wash and two proper coats before the siding itself starts to suffer.

Caulk is the first thing to go

On houses this age, the joints where trim meets siding open up before the paint fails. Water gets behind the boards there, and on hardboard or engineered wood siding that means swollen bottom edges. Recaulking every joint is part of our standard exterior scope.

Open ground on three sides

Lochbuie sits on flat land with farm fields around it. Steady wind dries paint too fast and blows dust into it, so we plan spray days around the forecast and switch to brush and roller when it is gusty.

Check your covenants

Several Lochbuie subdivisions have color rules and an approval step. We will help you find the approved palette and put together the request; see our HOA painting page.

Questions

Lochbuie painting questions

Do you paint houses in Lochbuie?

Yes, anywhere in Lochbuie, on both the Adams and Weld County sides. Interior and exterior painting, fences and decks, cabinets and drywall repair.

Why are so many Lochbuie houses fading at the same time?

Because they were built at the same time. About 71% of Lochbuie's homes went up after 1999, mostly with the same thin builder paint, so they reach the chalking stage together.

Is Lochbuie in Adams County or Weld County?

Both. The town crosses the county line. That makes no difference to painting, though it decides which county's records show your build year.

Do I need HOA approval to repaint in Lochbuie?

In some subdivisions, yes, sometimes even for the same color. Check your covenants before you pick colors; we can prepare the request.

Is there a travel fee for Lochbuie?

No. Lochbuie is next door to Brighton and inside our normal service area.
