## D. Verified facts (checked 2026-11-08)
- EPA RRP: applies to homes, child care, preschools built before 1978; paid contractors disturbing painted surfaces must be certified; sole proprietors included; owners on their own homes generally exempt; rentals and flips covered. https://www.epa.gov/lead/renovation-repair-and-painting-program (fetched). The 6 / 20 sq ft thresholds were not on that page: UNVERIFIED here.
- Brighton permits: I found no clear city statement on painting; the city FAQ says to call a permit technician at 303-655-2017 (1stop@brightonco.gov) if in doubt; a "Do I Need a Building Permit?" document exists but was not read. https://brightonco.gov/m/faq?cat=41 ; https://www.brightonco.gov/DocumentCenter/View/37928 (search results, not fetched).
